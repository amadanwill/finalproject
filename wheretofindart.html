See Albers in NYC — Pratt Student Museum Guide

Through the Student Government Association (SGA), Pratt students are able to receive complimentary general admission to leading museums in New York City. This guide will help you locate Albers’ works across the city and reserve your free student tickets with your valid Pratt ID.

Partner NYC Museums & Collections

Explore participating institutions in New York City housing Josef Albers' works.

The Whitney Museum of American Art Holds 123 works (115 prints, 6 paintings, and 2 drawings) Homage to the Square: Ascending No
MoMA Holds 243 works (paintings, prints, Bauhaus furniture, glass, and photography) Homage to the Square: Silent Hall Yes

Key Reminders Before Your Visit

  • Use Your Pratt Email: When reserving tickets online in advance, register using your official @pratt.edu email address.
  • Valid Pratt ID Required: Always bring your physical Pratt ID card with a current semester sticker for entry.
  • General Admission Coverage: Student memberships cover standard gallery admission; special ticketed exhibitions may carry separate fees.
